# The estate's legal surface, kept legible and honest

Legal & Contact is the group that holds the estate's legal and contact pages — the ones every site has and most write to be unread. The estate writes them to be read: in plain language, stating what is actually done rather than what a template says, and disclosing the uncomfortable parts rather than burying them. Its eight pages cover contact, press and media, privacy, terms, the advertising policy, cookies and local storage, intellectual property, and the sitemap. Two of these pages — cookies and sitemap — are thin-by-nature and exempt from the estate's word floor under DEF-2026-043, because padding a cookie policy to twelve hundred words would be the busywork harm the License names.

## Why the legal surface is written to be read

A privacy policy no one reads is a privacy policy that hides. The estate's legal pages are written against that: the privacy page states exactly what is collected (very little, and no third-party analytics), the advertising policy discloses the ad slots that keep the estate's own harm statement unremedied, and the cookies page states plainly that the estate uses local storage for session state and nothing more. The honesty is not decorative — under the License's relational stratum, a term a person cannot understand is a term they cannot consent to.

## The map — what lives in this group

**Contact** is how to reach the curator and file a correction. **Press & Media** carries the estate's factual description for citation. **Privacy** states what is collected and what is not. **Terms** state the conditions of use, governed by ML-2.3. **Advertising Policy** discloses the AdSense and Adsterra slots and links them to the estate's unremedied HX-01 and HX-04 marks. **Cookies & Local Storage** is a thin-by-nature page stating the estate's minimal client-side storage. **Intellectual Property** states how the estate's content is licensed. **Sitemap** points to the human-readable route index at /search/.

## The worked instance — the advertising policy

The advertising policy is the group's most consequential page, because it is where the estate's money model meets its own License. It discloses that the content plane carries AdSense and Adsterra slots, states that no behavioural profiling or third-party analytics run alongside them, and links directly to the estate's Part H Statement, where HX-01 (attention expropriation) and HX-04 (the human as product) are marked unremedied because of exactly these slots. The policy does not defend the slots as harmless; it names them as the reason the estate fails two of its own absolutes, and states that removing them is the available remedy.

## Limits of this group

These pages state the estate's actual practice and intent; they are not legal advice and are drafted by a curator who is not a lawyer, a fact the terms page states rather than hides. Two pages are thin-by-nature and say so. And the advertising disclosure describes the demo's current state — if the ad slots are removed, these pages and the harm statement must both be updated together, or the estate has created a stale-disclosure defect against itself.

## The falsifier for this group

The group's governing claim is that legal pages written to be read and disclosing the uncomfortable parts serve a reader better than boilerplate. The refutation: if readers of these pages understand the estate's actual practices no better than readers of a standard template, the plain-language effort adds nothing.

## What this group obliges

Read the advertising policy if you want to understand why the estate holds two unremedied marks against its own License. Read the privacy page to confirm what little is collected. And use the sitemap at /search/ as the human-readable index and no-JavaScript fallback for the whole estate.
