# MacroLifeTech — the research umbrella, written MacroLifeTech in every instance

Meta-X is the display label for **MacroLifeTech (Meta-X)**, the civilizational research umbrella of the estate. The name is MacroLifeTech, spelled that way in every instance in prose, in metadata, and in the search index; the older misspelling survives in exactly one place, the legacy redirect, and nowhere else. Meta-X is a research-preview pillar: nothing under it is taught as operator craft, and every strong claim carries a citation and a charter that states what the research will and will not do. Its four groups are the Umbrella, the Nine Programs, BCIA, and the Honesty Layer.

## Why this pillar exists and why it is gated

MacroLifeTech asks what computation becomes when it stops being confined to silicon and starts touching living, embodied, and collective systems. That is a genuinely speculative frontier, and treating it as anything other than research would be dishonest, so the whole pillar is research-preview and its most advanced program, BCIA, is gated. The gating is not marketing scarcity; it is a real condition, because BCIA assumes the Ascent ladder's substrate literacy and carries an explicit human-cell exclusion that a casual reader must not be able to stumble past. Meta-X exists to hold civilizational-scale questions to the same evidentiary discipline the rest of the estate uses on ranking algorithms — which means stating the falsifier, dating the claim, and publishing the audit.

## The map — what lives inside Meta-X

**The Umbrella** states what MacroLifeTech is, its research charter, what it is explicitly not, its method-and-evidence policy, and its publication and preprint policy. **The Nine Programs** are the research lines — Agora (collective decision), Ma'nā (meaning and semantics), Metabolism (energy and flow), Soma (embodiment), Paideia (formation), Athar (trace and record), Āfāq (horizons), Mīzān (measure and balance), and BCIA (bio-computational) — each a program page. **BCIA** is the deepest and most governed group: what it is and is not, the eight series, the volume roadmap, Document Zero and its seven bases, the charter with the human-cell exclusion, the cloister/atrium governance, the notation, the bibliography, the contribution guidelines, and the locked gateway. **The Honesty Layer** is BCIA's public status: remediation, the critical audit, the publication-readiness protocol, the defect register, and the scope limits and open problems.

## How a Meta-X page holds itself honest

Every Meta-X page runs at three altitudes. At the micro level it states a mechanism and a worked instance from the published literature. At the meta level it states its evidence class — which across this pillar is deliberately low — its decay interval, and the observation that would falsify it. At the macro level it places the research in the civilizational frame the License's Part H maps: what a technology touching living systems would do to the successor, to the record, and to the option set of parties not present to consent. The BCIA charter's human-cell exclusion is the sharpest expression of this: it is a red line stated in advance, before any result exists to tempt the research across it.

## The worked frame — the honesty layer

BCIA's honesty layer is the pillar's clearest instance of method. Rather than present BCIA as a finished research program, the estate publishes a critical audit of it — a document that argues against BCIA's own readiness — alongside a remediation status, a publication-readiness protocol that must be satisfied before anything is published as a finding, and a defect register that lists BCIA's own open problems. A reader arriving at BCIA does not meet a brochure; they meet a program that has written down, in public, the reasons it is not yet ready. That inversion is what a research-preview pillar owes, and it is why the demo strip is forced on every BCIA page.

## Limits of this pillar

Meta-X reports no primary results; it reads, connects, and charters. It cannot tell you that any of its nine programs will succeed, because that is unknown, and any page claiming otherwise would violate the pillar's own method policy. BCIA is gated and will remain so until its publication-readiness protocol is satisfied. The Arabic text is authoritative for Meta-X and BCIA under the estate's language-authority rule, which means the English pages are translations of equal editorial standing but not the citable source — a direct application of the License's language-parity duty.

## The falsifier for the pillar

Meta-X's governing claim is that civilizational research becomes safer, not weaker, when its red lines and its audit are published before its results. The refutation: if publishing the human-cell exclusion and the critical audit in advance demonstrably slows nothing harmful and only signals virtue, then the honesty layer is HX-15 conduct — compliance theatre — and it must be replaced with enforceable structure rather than disclosure.

## What this pillar asks of you

Read the umbrella's "what this is not" page before the program pages; it is where Meta-X tells you the ambitions it refuses. Treat the BCIA honesty layer as the real front door to BCIA, not the roadmap. And carry the pillar's epistemic humility forward under ML-2.3 Part H: a research-preview claim laundered into a product certainty is exactly the successor-foreclosing move the register's Stratum VI describes.
