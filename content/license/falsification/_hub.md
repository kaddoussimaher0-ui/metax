# The license held to the standard it demands of everything else

This group is the license's own honesty layer. An instrument built on falsifiability that did not publish its own objections, withdrawals, defects, and open questions would be committing the harm it names in HX-15 — adopting an ethics instrument as a shield rather than as change. This group refuses that. It carries the objections raised against the license and the responses to them, the clauses that have been withdrawn or narrowed, the defect log of the license's own faults, the changelog from ML-2.2 to ML-2.3, the superseded ML-2.2 kept for the record, and the open questions still awaiting a scholar's or a lawyer's ruling.

## Why a license keeps its own defect log

The estate's rule is that a correction outsiders cannot see into is not a correction. Applied to the license, this means the instrument must publish where it has been wrong and where it is unsure. The move from fourteen harms to twenty-five in a single cycle, for example, is itself flagged as a risk in the drafting: a register that grows that fast, with no visible threshold, is the exact artefact HX-15 describes. Publishing that self-criticism — and the amendment procedure that caps future growth at three harms per cycle — is how the license demonstrates it is not doing to itself what it warns others against.

## The map — what lives in this group

Each leaf is a record. **Objections & Responses** reproduces the strongest criticisms of the license and answers them in the open. **Withdrawals & Narrowings** lists clauses the license has retreated on, with reasons. **Defect Log (ML-D-nnn)** carries the license's own faults by identifier, including the open bibliography defect that only a human with the sources can close. **Changelog 2.2 → 2.3** states what changed and why, including the seven-absolutes-of-twenty-five ratio and the new roles, scale gate, and breach machinery. **ML-2.2 (superseded)** is the prior version, retained with a superseded header rather than deleted. **Open Questions (RAT-nn)** lists the ratifications still pending, including whether the Islamic-ethics mappings hold as a matter of fiqh.

## The worked frame — the open bibliography defect

The clearest instance of the group's honesty is a defect the estate refuses to close. Part H's evidence must be checkable, which requires a full bibliography, and the register currently cites author-year without complete references. The defect is filed as open — and it is marked as one that must not be closed by a machine, because a machine-generated reference list in a document whose whole claim is checkable evidence would be the harm HX-03 describes, committed by the register against itself. So the license leaves the defect open, names why, and waits for a human with the sources. That is what refusing to launder a gap looks like.

## Limits of this group

These pages record the license's history and faults; they do not resolve them. An open defect is open until its resolution note says otherwise, and an open question is open until a qualified authority answers it. The superseded ML-2.2 is retained for reference and is explicitly not in force. And the objections reproduced here are the strongest the estate has found, not the only ones possible — a reader with a better objection is invited to file it through the corrections log.

## The falsifier for this group

The governing claim is that publishing the license's own objections and defects makes it more trustworthy than a license that hides them. The refutation: if adopters shown the defect log and open questions trust the license less, and comply less, than adopters shown only the finished instrument, then the honesty layer is a cost without a benefit.

## What this group asks of you

Read the objections before you decide the license is sound; the estate would rather you adopt it knowing its weaknesses than reject it later for a fault it had already published. If you find a defect the log does not carry, file it — the estate commits to publishing your report and its response. And treat the open questions as genuinely open: where the license marks a ruling as pending, it is not asking you to trust an answer it has not got.
