## Why this exists

The best teachers in any field are rarely the loudest. They are practitioners with hard-won judgment and no interest in running a content business. The Academies Community exists to give those people a structure: a way to turn a defensible thesis into a teaching institution without having to invent the standard, the credentialing, the governance, or the platform from scratch. You bring the expertise; the community brings the scaffolding that keeps it honest.

It is also how MetaX itself scales without diluting. Rather than one school trying to be authoritative about everything, the universe grows as a federation of focused academies, each accountable for its own territory and each held to the same non-negotiable DNA.

## What you bring

- A **thesis** — a defensible claim about a territory you know deeply. Not a topic you find interesting; a claim you could defend under informed criticism and would stake your name on.
- A **curator** — you, or someone explicitly accountable for the standard of the academy. Accountability has to have a name attached to it.
- A willingness to work to the **shared standard of mastery** — decay-aware, falsifiable, governed, version-controlled — even when it is inconvenient, which it will sometimes be.

## What the community brings

- The **Terms of Top Levels** — the standard and the ladder that carries an academy from an audience to a full institution, with the requirements for each level made explicit rather than left to politics.
- A network of interlinked future academies, so learners can move between territories without leaving the universe or re-learning the ground rules.
- The same **DNA** every MetaX pillar inherits: decay-aware claims, falsifiable theses, governance as architecture, and public version control with no silent edits.
- The credentialing layer, so your learners earn defensible mastery — portfolio, verified outcomes, peer review — rather than a certificate that means "attended."

## The five levels

The founding path is a ladder of five levels, and no level is skippable:

1. **L1 Applicant** — you submit a thesis and a curator. This is where the countdown above matters: the first formal review window opens on that date.
2. **L2 Sandbox** — you build and publish a small body of work to the standard, in a contained space, to prove the thesis survives contact with reality.
3. **L3 Provisional** — your academy operates publicly under supervision, with its claims subject to the same audit as everyone else's.
4. **L4 Chartered** — a recognised academy in the universe, accountable for its territory.
5. **L5 Institution** — the summit: a self-governing institution that has proven its standard over time.

Nothing on this ladder survives on legacy authority alone. An academy climbs by producing published, falsifiable work that survives scrutiny — the same way TopTech earned its place, one defensible claim at a time.

## The road to institution

An academy is admitted as a charter, proves its standard through published, falsifiable work, and climbs the Terms of Top Levels toward institution status. There is no shortcut, and there is no permanent tenure: a level once reached still has to be maintained, because a claim that is no longer defended is a claim that has quietly decayed. The eleven requirement groups that define what "meeting the standard" actually means are published in full, so there is no hidden bar and no moving goalposts.
