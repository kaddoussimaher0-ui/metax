MetaX.Academy is no longer a single school. As of v2026.08 it is a **universe with three pillars**, each its own world with its own curriculum, governance and future — bound by one shared standard of mastery. This release note explains what changed, why it changed, and what it means for anyone learning or building inside the universe.

## The three pillars

1. **[TopTech Academy](toptech.html)** — the operator curriculum for the machine-mediated internet: search and retrieval, owned audience, paid media, analytics, AI operations, agentic commerce and more. This is where MetaX began, and it remains the pillar with the most shipped material.
2. **[Academies Community](community.html)** — a system that lets a subject-matter expert found their own academy under the Terms of Top Levels, climbing a defined ladder from a chartered thesis to a full institution.
3. **[MacroLifeTach](macrolifetach.html)** — the Meta-X research umbrella exploring a living computational substrate to replace silent silicon, governed from the first moment rather than as an afterthought.

Together they form a single universe with three entry points. You can enter as an operator learning to survive the machine-mediated internet, as a founder building your own academy, or as a reader following the research pillar — and whichever door you use, you meet the same standard on the other side.

## Why expand from one school to three?

The honest reason is that the original TopTech thesis kept implying larger questions. Teaching operators to build companies that survive platform migrations raised the question of how you build a *teaching institution* that survives — which is the Academies Community. And teaching people to operate on top of silicon infrastructure raised the question of what happens when silicon itself hits its limits — which is the Ascent and, beyond it, MacroLifeTach. Rather than smuggle those questions into TopTech and dilute it, we gave each its own pillar with honest, separate status.

Crucially, the expansion does **not** mean everything is now claimed as finished. TopTech's Operator Craft is live. The engineering branch is mostly planned. The Ascent is a research preview. The BCIA gateway is gated behind an honesty layer. Making the universe bigger did not lower the bar; it made the bar apply in more places, each with a truthful label.

## One DNA across all three

Whatever pillar you enter, the same non-negotiable principles apply. This shared DNA is what makes it a universe rather than three unrelated brands sharing a logo:

- **Decay-aware** — every claim is classed Durable, Semi-durable, or Perishable and dated, so you always know its expected shelf life.
- **Falsifiable** — explicit nulls and a decision rule, so a claim can actually be shown wrong.
- **Governed** — ethics and compliance as architecture, not an afterthought bolted on at the end.
- **Version-controlled** — public patch notes for every release. No silent edits. Ever.

## Status badges, everywhere

With this release we also standardised how status is communicated across the whole universe. Every series, branch, and pillar now carries one of four honest badges:

- **live** — shipped to the full quality gate and available now.
- **planned** — committed to, openly labelled as not-yet-built, with no pretence otherwise.
- **research-preview** — genuinely exploratory material shared for feedback, not settled doctrine.
- **gated** — available only behind an explicit honesty layer, because the topic demands it.

You will see these badges in the mega-menu, on the [TopTech architecture](toptech.html#branches-sec) page, and throughout the curriculum. They exist so you are never confused about whether something is a finished course or a research sketch.

> One universe, three entry points, a single standard of mastery.

## What this means for you

If you were following TopTech, nothing you were learning has been taken away or diluted — it has simply been placed in a clearer structure with a bigger horizon around it. If you are new, start with the [Start Here](start-here.html) diagnostic, which routes you to the right pillar for what you are trying to do. And if you want to build rather than only learn, the Academies Community is now open for charters.

Courses continue to ship in waves — see the [course rollout roadmap](article.html?id=course-rollout-roadmap) for the schedule, and watch Academy News for each wave's release post. The universe is bigger now, but the promise is unchanged: version-controlled, or it is a lie.
