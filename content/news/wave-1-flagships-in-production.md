## What "in production" actually means

"In production" is not marketing speak for "coming soon someday." At MetaX it is a defined state with concrete entry criteria. For a course to be in production it must have:

- A locked outline of **12 courses × 6 lessons** (13 for SEO, which includes a Course 0 diagnostic gate).
- A first full draft of every lesson script, written to the *What is · Why · How · Lab · Artifact · Scenario Assessment* arc.
- Labs and downloadable artifacts being built and tested by an operator who did not write the lesson.
- A pre-registered hypothesis with a null and a decision rule, written *before* the results section — not reverse-engineered to look good afterwards.
- A scheduled review pass by a second operator against our published quality gate.

Only after all of that clears does a lesson flip to **live** on the [Full Curriculum](curriculum.html). Until then it stays honestly labelled as in production, so you are never sold a promise dressed as a finished product.

## Why the flagships come first

Both flagships were deliberately torn down and rebuilt to the same standard as the next-level stack — with kill criteria, unit economics, falsifiability, and an answer-engine spine. They set the bar. Publishing them first means every series that follows has a concrete, shipped reference for what "good" looks like, rather than a style guide nobody has actually had to meet.

- **SEO Mastery** covers the three-layer discipline of modern discovery: human-reader ranking, machine retrieval and citation across answer engines, and owned-audience conversion. It refuses the old fiction that SEO is one monolithic ranking game; in 2026 there are at least three overlapping games, and confusing them is the most common way operators waste a year.
- **Social Media Marketing** is the curriculum *underneath* the brand layer: attention economics, trust formation, and honest algorithm physics — not durability claims against "every algorithm change." We teach the mechanisms that persist (why distribution is granted, how trust compounds, where attention leaks) rather than tactics that a single feed-ranking update can erase.

## The quality gate, in detail

The flagships are the proof that the gate is real. Here is what each lesson survives before it reaches you:

1. **Falsifiability review.** Every strong claim must be phrased so that a specific observation could prove it wrong. Claims that cannot fail are cut or reclassified as opinion.
2. **Decay classification.** Each tactic is dated and marked Durable, Semi-durable, or Perishable, so you know its expected shelf life and when to re-check it.
3. **Lab reproduction.** A second operator runs the lab from the written instructions alone. If they cannot reproduce the artifact, the lab is rewritten.
4. **Unit-economics pass.** Any tactic that costs money or time is expressed as a cost-per-outcome, not a vibe. If it cannot pay for itself, that is stated plainly.
5. **Accessibility and compliance rails.** Captions, structure, and regulatory notes are embedded, not appended.
