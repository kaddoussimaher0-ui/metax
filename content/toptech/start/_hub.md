# The front door that places you rather than sells to you

Start & Placement is the honest entrance to TopTech. Rather than push the flashiest flagship at a newcomer, it does what a good academy does first: it places you where you actually are. It exists because most learning platforms sell a course before they know whether the learner needs it, and the estate refuses that. This group estimates where you already stand, states how the estate expects to be read, shows what must come before what, tells you the real hours involved, and names the promises TopTech will not make. Its five pages are the placement diagnostic, how to study here, the prerequisites map, the time budget and load, and what TopTech is not.

## Why placement comes before selling

A learner dropped at the wrong rung fails on a schedule they cannot read — too advanced and they drown, too basic and they leave. Placement fixes this by matching the learner to the rung, which means the estate sometimes tells a visitor they are not ready for what they came for, or that they already know it. That honesty costs a sale and buys a learner who succeeds, which is the trade the estate makes deliberately. The diagnostic is not a lead-capture form; it is a real estimate that drops you onto a concrete starting point with a status badge, so you always know whether what you are about to study is live, planned, research-preview, or gated.

## The map — what lives in this group

**Placement Diagnostic** estimates your current level and drops you onto a starting rung. **How to Study Here** states the estate's expectations — artefacts over minutes, the six-beat arc, the limits module. **Prerequisites Map** shows what must precede what across the series. **Time Budget & Load** states the real hours a series demands, not an aspirational figure. **What TopTech Is Not** names the promises the pillar refuses: no guaranteed timelines, no tactics without mechanism, no growth-hacking without cost accounting.

## The worked instance — the "what this is not" page

The "what this is not" page is the group's clearest instance of the estate's honesty. It states, in the negative, the things a vendor would hide: TopTech does not promise mastery on a fixed timeline, because mastery is evidenced not scheduled; it does not teach a tactic whose only defence is that it currently works; and it does not pretend the machine-mediated internet is neutral. Stating the refusals first is load-bearing, because the refusals are where the estate tells a learner the truth a marketer would omit.

## Limits of this group

The diagnostic estimates; it does not test — a precise placement still requires a learner to study and be assessed. The time budgets are honest estimates, not guarantees, and vary by learner. And the prerequisites map reflects the current catalog, which is itself partly scaffolding on a demo, so some prerequisites point at series not yet written, and their badges say so.

## The falsifier for this group

The group's governing claim is that placing a learner honestly produces better outcomes than selling them a course. The refutation: if learners who take the diagnostic and follow its placement succeed no more often than learners who pick a course at random, the placement machinery adds friction without benefit.

## What this group obliges

Take the diagnostic before you pick a series; the estate is designed to be entered where you are, not where the marketing points. Read the time budget honestly against your own available hours. And read the "what this is not" page as load-bearing — it is where TopTech tells you the truth that a vendor would hide, and it is the best test of whether the estate is worth your time.
