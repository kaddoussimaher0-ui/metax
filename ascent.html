🚫 What the Ascent is NOT
The Ascent is not a promise that biology will replace silicon, and not a claim that any living system today out-computes a datacentre. It is a disciplined study of substrate, limits, and one bridge — with the hype removed.
✕

Not a benchmark war

We do not publish like-for-like "N× more efficient than a GPU" claims. Different substrates are not directly comparable.

✕

Not settled doctrine

Everything here is a research preview. Read it to think, not to quote as fact.

✕

Not human-cell research
