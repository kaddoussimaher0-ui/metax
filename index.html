Nine pillars, one standard of mastery.

The pillars are not nine brands of the same thing. They carry distinct evidentiary standing — some live, some planned, some research-preview, some gated — and they share a single DNA: Sidq, falsifiability, declared limits, and correctability. Every card below resolves to a real hub page.

⊢ The shared DNA

What every MetaX pillar inherits.

Each property is stated with the condition under which it has failed, because a property with no failure condition is a slogan.

☉

Sidq

Truthfulness as the operative constraint. No page asserts more than its evidence supports; none conceals what would weaken it. Failed when a claim outlives its evidence.

🔬

Falsifiability

Every substantive claim carries what would refute it. Failed when a page makes a claim no reader could disprove even in principle.

⊣

Declared limits

Every body of teaching states what it does not cover and cannot do. Failed when a method is taught without stating where it stops working.

↺

Correctability

Any reader can file a defect; accepted and rejected corrections are published with reasons. Failed when a correction queue exists that outsiders cannot see into.
