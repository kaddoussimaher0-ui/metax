⊢ About · The Standard of Mastery

Mastery is judgement tested under scrutiny.

MetaX assesses one thing, and it is not recall, not seat-time, and not a percentage of a video watched. It is judgement tested under scrutiny — a real artefact, examined by a named reviewer against a published rubric, in a process the candidate could see.

How a Claim Is Checked → The Credential Tracks

A credential that cannot be checked by a stranger is a reputation, not a record. MetaX issues records.

▤ MX-001 §1.4 · The apparatus

Every substantive page carries five things.

The standard is not a slogan printed on a certificate — it is machinery that appears on every page, so that a reader with no account and no permission can find out whether a claim is wrong.

🔬

A claim card

States the falsifier — what would have to be true for the claim to be wrong. Governed by ST-04.

⏳

Decay class & date

Durable 36 months, Semi-durable 12, Perishable 3. Past the date, the page says so on its own face.

⚖

Objections at full strength

The strongest available counter-argument, not a straw version. Governed by ST-06.

◐

[VERIFY] tags

On anything checkable but unchecked — counted publicly in the Verification Log.

⊢

A page stamp

Version, last review, decay class, curator, and the instrument that governs the page. Every page, no exceptions.

Summaries never govern. Where a page summarises a governing text, it says so and names the text. The build fails if a summary asserts an obligation its governing text does not contain — because a summary that quietly adds an obligation has legislated by paraphrase.

✅ The operational definition

What a MetaX credential actually asserts.

A MetaX credential asserts that a named reviewer examined a real artefact produced by the candidate, against a published rubric, in a process the candidate could see — and could defend the resulting decision to a second calibrated reviewer who had not seen the first reviewer's reasoning.

👤

A named reviewer

Not an algorithm, not an anonymous panel. A person accountable for the decision.

🧩

A real artefact

Something the candidate made — assessed on evidence produced, never on minutes spent.

📐

A published rubric

The standard is visible before the work begins, not revealed after the verdict.

⇄

A second reviewer

The decision must survive a calibrated reviewer who never saw the first one's reasoning.

⟡ MX-005 · The four tracks

Four credential tracks, named once and for all.

These names prevail over any alternate shorthand or local variant. Each carries its own renewal rule, because a credential that never expires is making a durability claim it may not be able to keep.

1

Series Practitioner

Competence in a single series, evidenced by its capstone artefact. Does not expire.

2

Professional Operator

Craft across a branch of the curriculum. Renews at 36 months.

3

Master-Fellow

Mastery at the threshold of research. Does not expire, but remains under standing review.

4

MetaX Contributor

Standing as a contributor to the corpus itself. Renews at 12 months.

⚠ The honest boundary

And what a credential says nothing about.

The standard is narrow on purpose. A record that quietly implies more than it verified is a dark pattern in doctrinal clothing.

A MetaX credential asserts nothing about employability, income, or aptitude. It is not an accreditation, not a licence to practise, and not a prediction about a career. Any page on any MetaX property that implies otherwise is a defect and should be reported — and the correction, accepted or rejected, is published with its reasons.
