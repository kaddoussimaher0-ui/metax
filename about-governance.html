The powers placed beyond the Curator.

Maher is the founder, sole author of record, and final editorial authority of MetaX. Three powers are deliberately outside his reach — and their being outside his reach is the primary evidence that MetaX is an institution rather than a personality.

The Three Powers → Read the Charter · §0.5

◉ Charter §0.5 · Reserved powers

Three things the founder cannot do.

Each of these is a real constraint with a named process behind it, not a courtesy. §0.5 may not be amended to weaken any of them.

⚖

He cannot overrule the ethics veto

A designated ethics reviewer may block any publication on the grounds set out in MX-014, and there is no appeal to the Curator. The veto exists before the first publication, not after the first incident.

⊢

He cannot revoke a credential on his own authority

Revocation requires the review-panel process in MX-005 §5.9, with reasons published. A credential the Curator issued is not a credential the Curator can quietly withdraw.

⎇

He cannot unilaterally amend succession

Once a successor has accepted in writing, the succession instrument cannot be suspended or amended without the successor's countersignature or their documented refusal.

⬡ The governing roles

Who holds which authority.

Authority in the universe is distributed across named roles with published limits. No single seat, including the Curator's, holds all of it.

✍

The Curator

Founder and final editorial authority. Governs releases and holds the final editorial call — subject to the three reserved powers above.

⚖

The Ethics Reviewer

Holds a genuine veto over any publication under MX-014. The one authority the Curator cannot overrule.

⊢

The Review Panel

Owns credential revocation under MX-005 §5.9, and calibrates second-reviewer decisions so that no single reviewer's judgement is final.

⎇

The Successor

Named in MX-015. Once they accept in writing, the succession instrument can only change with their countersignature or documented refusal.

↺ Charter §0.6 · How the rules change

The Charter changes only in the open.

The Charter is amended only by a numbered release published in the Record, with reasons, a diff, and a 30-day comment window.

▤

A numbered release

No amendment happens off the record. Each carries a version number, the reasons for the change, and a public diff.

⏳

A 30-day window

Member academies at Level 3 and above may file objections during the comment window, and every objection must receive a reasoned reply.

🚫

Two clauses are locked

The three refusals (§0.4) and the reserved powers (§0.5) may never be amended to weaken them. Those two sections are the floor.
