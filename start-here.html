◉ The Status Legend

How to read our badges.

We never dress a plan up as a finished product. Every series, page and menu item carries one of four honest states.

🟢

Live

Shipped and maintained. You can study it now, and it carries a public changelog.

🟡

Planned

Scoped and on the roadmap, not yet written. The outline is public so you can see where it fits.

🔵

Research Preview

An honest work-in-progress from the frontier. Read it as a question, not a settled answer.

🔴

Gated

Under remediation or ethically sensitive. Access is deliberate and the honesty layer comes first.
