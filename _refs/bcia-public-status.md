# BCIA Public Status Page

> **Status:** UNDER REMEDIATION. Nothing on this page or in Branch D should be interpreted as BCIA doctrine.

## Why this page exists
TopTech may teach **toward** BCIA. It may not present BCIA's unresolved notation, taxonomy, governance contradictions, or unverified claims as settled.

## Seven remediation gates before Branch E can open
| Gate | Requirement | Current status | Why it matters |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Remove duplicate Volume I and publish one canonical inventory | Open | Prevents contradictory citations and false counts. |
| 2 | Reconcile the D1-D10 domain-tag system with the seven-layer BCIA taxonomy | Open | Avoids teaching two incompatible maps as one system. |
| 3 | Resolve conflicting definitions of AMN and LPN | Open | Core terminology cannot be unstable. |
| 4 | Publish a clear position on universality vs revelatory grounding | Open | Prevents doctrinal slippage and category confusion. |
| 5 | Resolve stewardship-vs-ownership contradiction in governance language | Open | Institutions need durable legal and moral stewardship rules. |
| 6 | Verify or retract date-specific and quantitative claims | Open | Removes unsourced authority signaling. |
| 7 | Freeze a canonical glossary, notation policy, and public errata workflow | Open | Gives contributors one language and one correction path. |

## What Branch D may say safely
- That silicon computation has physical, economic, and epistemic limits.
- That non-binary, fuzzy, biological, embodied, and networked computation are legitimate scientific topics.
- That BCIA remains gated until its own internal contradictions are remediated.

## What Branch D may not say
- That BCIA's internal notation is settled.
- That contested BCIA quantitative claims are verified.
- That any wet-lab, cultivation, synthetic-biology bench workflow, organism engineering, or human-cell work is part of this curriculum.

## Absolute exclusions
**Human-cell exclusion: absolute.**
**No wet-lab protocols. No substrate cultivation. No synthetic-biology bench instruction. No organism engineering.**

## Public-link policy
Every Branch D series includes a panel titled **“What BCIA claims here, and what is not yet settled”** and links back to this status page.
