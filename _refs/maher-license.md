## PART 0 — DECLARATION AND CHARACTER OF THIS INSTRUMENT

### 0.1 Nature of the Document

Maher's License ("this License", "ML-2.0", or "the License") is a single instrument performing four functions. It is (a) a **direct license** capable of governing software, source code, documentation, datasets, designs, and other expressive or functional works; (b) an **ethical-source license** placing conditions on use beyond those of conventional licenses; (c) a **meta-license** defining an objective rubric by which other licenses are evaluated; and now (d) a **governing charter** capable of governing *Works of Foundational Character* (§2.2), supplying the top-level ethical-legal order to which such Works and any licenses derived from this one must conform.

This four-fold character is deliberate. ML-2.0 does not merely add another license to the landscape; it supplies a **normative layer above** both the licensing ecosystem and the Works it governs — a principled standard against which existing licenses, derived licenses, and foundational Works alike may be measured — while remaining usable as a working license in its own right.

### 0.2 Honest Labeling (a requirement, not a disclaimer)

In keeping with **Sidq** (honesty and truthfulness), the drafters state plainly: **this License is not an "Open Source" license as defined by the Open Source Initiative, and is not a "Free Software" license as defined by the Free Software Foundation.** It is not, and is not intended to be, approved by either body. The reason is structural and disclosed openly: this License conditions use on ethical compliance (Part C) and now on charter conformity (Part G), which the Open Source Definition (criteria 5–6) and Free Software Definition (Freedom 0) prohibit. Concealing this would itself violate *Sidq*. Any party referencing this License must describe it accurately as a **governing ethical-source charter and meta-license**, and must not represent it as OSI- or FSF-approved absent written approval.

### 0.3 Source-Available, Ethical-Source, and Charter Classification

ML-2.0 occupies the category of **source-available, ethical-source, governing charters**, distinguished by (a) its explicit grounding in a named ethical-legal tradition, (b) its meta-licensing function, and (c) its charter layer for Works of Foundational Character. It preserves the core liberties to read, study, modify, and redistribute the Source Form (for software) or the authoritative form (for expressive/foundational Works), subject to Parts B, C, and G. The intent remains: **maximally permissive within ethical bounds**.

### 0.4 The Self-Hosted Registry Model

This License is published and maintained through the self-hosted **Registry** (§E.4), the canonical source of the text, version history, Compatibility Determinations (Part D), the evaluated-licenses list with recorded pros and cons, and any Charter Conformity Determinations (Part G). The Registry — not OSI, SPDX, or any third party — is the governing authority for interpretation, versioning, and administration.

## PART 1 — PREAMBLE AND ETHICAL-LEGAL FOUNDATION

### 1.1 Purpose of the Preamble

This Preamble is integral and operative, not recital. Ambiguities are resolved per §11 in the manner most consistent with the Foundational Principles, which are simultaneously the *spirit* and the *interpretive key* of the instrument.

### 1.2 The Four Foundational Principles

Carried forward unchanged from Version 1.1: **Amanah** (trustworthiness and faithful stewardship), **Sidq** (honesty and truthfulness), **Adl** (justice, fairness, and proportionality), and **Shafafiyah** (transparency and openness), each as defined in §1.2 of the prior versions and presented as universal moral norms requiring conduct, not belief.

### 1.3 Subsidiary Ethical Norms

**(i) Prevention of Darar (harm).** *Lā ḍarar wa-lā ḍirār.* Carried forward unchanged.

**(ii) Prohibition of Zulm (oppression and exploitation).** Carried forward unchanged.

**(iii) Avoidance of Gharar (excessive uncertainty/deception in dealings).** Carried forward unchanged.

**(iv) Prohibition of Riba (usurious and exploitative increase).** *[Elevated — Version 2.0.]* *Riba* — literally "increase" or "growth" — denotes an unlawful, unearned, risk-free increase extracted by virtue of the structure of a dealing rather than by genuine, risk-bearing economic activity. In Version 1.1 this norm was an interpretive value only. In Version 2.0, by the Steward's deliberate elevation, *Riba* in its **core, defined form (§C.9)** is an **operative, absolute, non-severable prohibition** that the License refuses absolutely. This elevation is bounded precisely by §C.9 and reconciled with the general permission for lawful finance in §C.8, so that the prohibition reaches only the defined core of exploitative *riba* and never ordinary, lawful, risk-bearing financial or commercial activity.

### 1.4 Universality and Non-Coercion of Belief

*Lā ikrāha fī al-dīn.* Carried forward unchanged: no user need adopt any belief; compliance is a matter of conduct.

### 1.5 Relationship to Secular and International Law; Reinforcing Character

Carried forward unchanged from Version 1.1: the Ethical-Use Conditions are primarily a **reinforcing and remedial layer**; no greater enforceability is claimed than the forum's law grants; structural and technical safeguards, not this License, are the primary protection; this License is the fallback. **Exception:** the core *Riba* prohibition of §C.9 is, by the Steward's election, asserted as a substantive condition of the grant to the maximum extent any forum will enforce it, and as a charter-level refusal under Part G regardless of forum enforceability — see §C.9.5.

**B.6.5 Sensitive-Material Redaction — Minimal and Justified.** *[Revised — Version 2.0.]* The source-availability obligation does not require disclosure of private keys, user data, or genuine operational secrets whose disclosure would itself cause harm (*Darar*) or breach a trust (*Amanah*). ### C.9 Absolute Prohibition of Core Riba — *[New, operative, Version 2.0]*

**C.9.1 Status.** This Section establishes the single elevated absolute prohibition of Version 2.0. It is a **non-severable condition of every grant** and a **charter-level refusal** under Part G. It is to be read together with, and as the sole exception to the breadth of permission in, §C.8. Nothing outside the bounded definition in §C.9.2 is reached by this Section.

**C.9.2 Definition of Core Riba (the international, license-grade definition).** "**Core Riba**" means a transaction or instrument bearing **all** of the following cumulative elements:

> **(a) A monetary or fungible-of-the-same-kind base.** The dealing concerns money, currency, or a fungible commodity exchanged for more of the same kind, or a loan/advance of money or such fungible; and
>
> **(b) A pre-fixed, guaranteed increase.** The provider is entitled, by the structure of the dealing, to a **predetermined increase over the principal** that is fixed or determinable in advance and **guaranteed irrespective of the actual outcome** of any underlying real economic activity; and
>
> **(c) Absence of genuine risk-bearing or value-creating counter-performance.** The increase is **not** the return on a genuine, risk-bearing investment, nor the price or profit of a real asset, service, or value actually transferred, produced, or borne by the provider; the provider assumes no genuine commercial risk of loss of principal arising from the venture, and performs no real economic activity commensurate with the increase. The increase accrues by virtue of **deferral, delay, or the mere lending of money** (*riba al-nasī'ah*), or by virtue of an **unequal exchange of the same fungible kind** (*riba al-faḍl*); and
>
> **(d) Extraction from a counterparty.** The increase is extracted from a borrower or counterparty, transferring value to the provider without corresponding risk borne or value given.

A transaction is Core Riba **only if all of (a)–(d) are present together.** The absence of any one element removes the transaction from this Section entirely, whereupon §C.8's general permission governs.

**C.9.3 What Core Riba is NOT (express exclusions, for certainty — *Adl*).** For the avoidance of all doubt, the following are **not** Core Riba and are **not** prohibited by this Section: (i) **profit** from genuine trade — buying and selling real assets at a margin; (ii) **rent or lease** income on a real asset whose risk the provider bears; (iii) **profit- and loss-sharing** returns (equity-like participation) where the provider genuinely bears risk of loss; (iv) **fees for actual services** genuinely rendered (administration, custody, validation, processing), priced for the service and not as a disguised time-value-of-money charge; (v) **yield, staking, or validation rewards** that compensate genuine effort, service, or risk rather than the mere lending of value; (vi) **demurrage or negative-rate mechanisms** that are charges for holding or storage, not increases extracted on a loan; (vii) any return where the provider bears genuine risk of loss of principal from the underlying venture. Where a structure is ambiguous as between Core Riba and a permitted form, and a good-faith, risk-bearing, value-creating construction is available, **that construction is presumed and the transaction is permitted** (§1.3(iv) interpretive supremacy of permission applies *only* to the boundary, never to dissolve a transaction that squarely meets all of §C.9.2(a)–(d)).

**C.9.4 Refusal.** A Work may not be used, nor knowingly permitted to be used (knowledge standard per §C.6), to operate, issue, enforce, or principally facilitate **Core Riba** as the designed and intended function of that use. The License grants **no rights** for such use; it is outside the scope of the grant, void, and a **material, non-curable breach** under §9.3.

**C.9.5 Charter-Level Refusal Independent of Forum.** Distinct from the rest of Part C (which §1.5 frames as merely reinforcing law), the Steward elects that the **refusal of Core Riba is asserted at the charter level** (Part G) as a matter of the License's own normative order, **whether or not** any particular forum would independently enforce it. A Work or Derived License whose designed purpose is the operation of Core Riba is **non-conforming to the Charter** (§G) and may not be represented as governed by, compatible with, or derived from this License. This is the one place where the License asserts a prohibition as an end in itself and not merely as a reinforcement of external law; the drafters disclose this elevation openly (*Sidq*) and confine it strictly to the bounded definition of §C.9.2.

**C.9.6 Non-Severability.** §C.9 may not be removed, weakened, or contracted around by any distribution, sublicensing, relicensing, dual-licensing, combination, or Derived License. Any purported instrument that strips or dilutes §C.9 is void and conveys no rights, and is itself non-conforming to the Charter.

## PART G — THE CHARTER LAYER (GOVERNING WORKS OF FOUNDATIONAL CHARACTER) — *[New, Version 2.0]*

### G.1 Purpose and Supremacy

This Part supplies the top-level governing order for Works of Foundational Character (§2.2) and for all Derived Licenses. Within the scope of this License, the Charter Layer is **supreme over the direct-license layer**: where a Work functions to establish or govern an order, the manner of that establishment must itself conform to the Foundational Principles. The Charter Layer does not enlarge the legal claims of Part C beyond §1.5 (save for the elected charter-level refusal of Core Riba, §C.9.5); rather, it states the normative standard to which foundational Works and derived instruments must conform to be entitled to invoke this License.

### G.2 Charter Conditions for Works of Foundational Character

A Work of Foundational Character governed by this License must, in its own substance and operation:

**(a) Truthful establishment (Sidq).** Not establish or propagate, as foundational, claims known by the author to be false, nor misrepresent the basis, authorship, or authority of the order it establishes; and label its own character honestly (including, where applicable, §0.2).

**(b) Just ordering (Adl).** Not order knowledge, value, or conduct in a manner designed to entrench unjust advantage, oppression (*Zulm*), or the exploitation of those subject to the order.

**(c) Transparent foundation (Shafafiyah).** Make the basis of the order it establishes available for study and good-faith critique by those subject to it, to the extent consistent with lawful confidentiality and §B.6.5.

**(d) Trustworthy stewardship (Amanah).** Be stewarded as a trust for those it governs, not as an instrument of private capture against them; and pass stewardship only to those who will maintain it consistent with these Conditions (cf. §E.5).

**(e) Non-harm (Darar) and non-Riba (§C.9).** Not be designed to inflict unlawful harm, and not be designed to operate, mandate, or principally facilitate Core Riba.

### G.3 Charter Conformity Determinations

The Steward may issue, by the same reasoned, published, good-faith procedure as Part D (§D.4–§D.5, applied *mutatis mutandis*), Charter Conformity Determinations classifying a Work of Foundational Character or a Derived License as **Charter-Conforming** or **Charter-Non-Conforming**, with a written statement of reasons mapped to §G.2 and a pros/cons record. Such Determinations are advisory classifications within this License's framework, are not legal judgments, and bind only the entitlement to invoke and be associated with this License.

### G.4 Consequence of Non-Conformity

A Work or Derived License determined Charter-Non-Conforming may not represent itself as governed by, compatible with, derived from, or endorsed by this License or its Steward, and any rights purportedly granted under this License for the non-conforming aspect are void to that extent. Non-conformity of one aspect does not void rights validly exercised in good-faith reliance as to conforming aspects (cf. §E.4 reliance protection and §14 severability).

### G.5 Humility Clause (Adl and Sidq toward critique)

Consistent with the Foundational Principles, the Steward acknowledges that the Charter Layer is itself a human instrument open to error and to good-faith critique and improvement. A Charter Conformity Determination must engage critique on its merits, must afford the affected party a fair opportunity to respond (§D.4(d) applied here), and must be revised upon sound correction. No Determination may be issued in bad faith, for the suppression of legitimate dissent, or to entrench the Steward's advantage against those the Charter is meant to serve — for that would itself violate *Adl* and *Amanah* and would render the Determination void.
