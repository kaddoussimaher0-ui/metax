# Public Canon Patch Sentences

These are minimal paragraphs in corpus register, drafted to close the six gaps identified in the review memo. They are additions only and are not presented as already ratified law. Source: [MetaX - Deep review](https://www.genspark.ai/api/files/s/dj7NUKdf).

## 1. Enforceability two-track reading

The ethical conditions of the corpus are read in two tracks. The first is the court-facing track: the terms most likely to survive as enforceable legal conditions under the governing law. The second is the charter-facing track: the social and institutional norms by which MetaX governs membership, publication, review, access, and standing even where a court would treat a term as non-enforceable. Any public page summarising obligations must state this distinction plainly and must not imply that every charter norm is equally judicially enforceable.

## 2. Degraded-mode continuity on steward incapacity

On death, adjudicated incapacity, or a declared continuity event, all agents enter read-only archival mode by default. They may continue to retrieve, index, and report, but they may not publish, alter governing documents, or create new binding artefacts until a successor authorized under the recorded continuity instrument accedes through the pre-committed authentication method named in the Registry.

## 3. Privacy classification boundary

Public by design applies to curriculum, governance, public status pages, and published determinations. Learner evidence, reviewer notes, ON REQUEST material, and personal data are private by default and become public only by explicit, revocable consent or by a stated retention and registry rule already disclosed in the governing instruments.

## 4. Language hierarchy rule

Arabic is canonical for the book library and English is canonical for the legal and governance instruments, except where an instrument declares a different prevailing text for itself. Where a bilingual page combines book matter and legal matter, the page must state which portion is governed by which canonical language and must not hide a material divergence behind translation convenience.

## 5. ToS / License mirror clause

Any permission or restriction stated in MX-002 concerning machine reading, indexing, retrieval, training, or synthetic derivation must mirror License §C.10 exactly in substance and in scope. If a discrepancy appears, the page is defective until harmonised by a numbered release and the inconsistency is entered in the defects register.

## 6. BCIA public status link sentence

BCIA remains under active remediation and its status is public. Any About, branch, or gateway page that names BCIA must link the current public status page so that no reader is asked to infer settledness where the project itself declares open gates.
