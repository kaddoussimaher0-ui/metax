## PART 0 — DECLARATION AND CHARACTER OF THIS INSTRUMENT

### 0.1 Nature of the Document

Maher's License ("this License", "ML-2.1", or "the License") is a single instrument
performing four functions. It is (a) a **direct license** capable of governing software,
source code, documentation, datasets, designs, and other expressive or functional works;
(b) an **ethical-source license** placing conditions on use beyond those of conventional
licenses; (c) a **meta-license** defining an objective rubric by which other licenses are
evaluated; and (d) a **governing charter** capable of governing *Works of Foundational
Character* ([§2.2](#22-defined-terms)), supplying the top-level ethical-legal order to which
such Works and any licenses derived from this one must conform.

This four-fold character is deliberate. This License does not merely add another license to
the landscape; it supplies a **normative layer above** both the licensing ecosystem and the
Works it governs — a principled standard against which existing licenses, derived licenses,
and foundational Works alike may be measured — while remaining usable as a working license in
its own right.

### 0.2 Honest Labeling (a requirement, not a disclaimer)

In keeping with **Sidq** (honesty and truthfulness), the drafters state plainly: **this
License is not an "Open Source" license as defined by the Open Source Initiative, and is not
a "Free Software" license as defined by the Free Software Foundation.** It is not, and is not
intended to be, approved by either body. The reason is structural and disclosed openly: this
License conditions use on ethical compliance (Part C) and on charter conformity (Part G),
which the Open Source Definition (criteria 5–6) and the Free Software Definition (Freedom 0)
prohibit. Concealing this would itself violate *Sidq*. Any party referencing this License
must describe it accurately as a **governing ethical-source charter and meta-license**, and
must not represent it as OSI- or FSF-approved absent written approval.

### 0.3 Source-Available, Ethical-Source, and Charter Classification

This License occupies the category of **source-available, ethical-source, governing
charters**, distinguished by (a) its explicit grounding in a named ethical-legal tradition,
(b) its meta-licensing function, and (c) its charter layer for Works of Foundational
Character. It preserves the core liberties to read, study, modify, and redistribute the
Source Form (for software) or the authoritative form (for expressive and foundational Works),
subject to Parts B, C, and G. The intent remains: **maximally permissive within ethical
bounds.**

The liberty to **study** is load-bearing and is relied upon in
[§C.10.1](#c10--machine-learning-model-training-and-synthetic-derivation).

### 0.4 The Self-Hosted Registry Model

This License is published and maintained through the self-hosted **Registry**
([§E.4](#e4-the-registry), [§E.7](#e7--registry-continuity-and-canonical-text-redundancy)),
the canonical source of the text, version history, Compatibility Determinations (Part D), the
evaluated-licenses list with recorded pros and cons, and any Charter Conformity
Determinations (Part G). The Registry — not OSI, SPDX, or any third party — is the governing
authority for interpretation, versioning, and administration, subject to the continuity and
redundancy requirements of §E.7.

## PART 1 — PREAMBLE AND ETHICAL-LEGAL FOUNDATION

### 1.1 Purpose of the Preamble

This Preamble is integral and operative, not recital. Ambiguities are resolved per §11 in the
manner most consistent with the Foundational Principles, which are simultaneously the *spirit*
and the *interpretive key* of the instrument.

### 1.2 The Four Foundational Principles

Carried forward unchanged: **Amanah** (trustworthiness and faithful stewardship), **Sidq**
(honesty and truthfulness), **Adl** (justice, fairness, and proportionality), and
**Shafafiyah** (transparency and openness) — each presented as a universal moral norm
requiring conduct, not belief.

### 1.3 Subsidiary Ethical Norms

**(i) Prevention of Darar (harm).** *Lā ḍarar wa-lā ḍirār.* Carried forward unchanged.

**(ii) Prohibition of Zulm (oppression and exploitation).** Carried forward unchanged.

**(iii) Avoidance of Gharar (excessive uncertainty or deception in dealings).** Carried
forward unchanged.

**(iv) Prohibition of Riba (usurious and exploitative increase).** *Riba* — literally
"increase" or "growth" — denotes an unlawful, unearned, risk-free increase extracted by virtue
of the structure of a dealing rather than by genuine, risk-bearing economic activity. In its
**core, defined form ([§C.9](#c9--absolute-prohibition-of-core-riba))** it is an **operative,
absolute prohibition** that this License refuses absolutely. The prohibition is bounded
precisely by §C.9.2 and reconciled with the general permission for lawful finance in §C.8, so
that it reaches only the defined core of exploitative *riba* and never ordinary, lawful,
risk-bearing financial or commercial activity.

### 1.4 Universality and Non-Coercion of Belief

*Lā ikrāha fī al-dīn.* No user need adopt any belief; compliance is a matter of conduct.

### 1.5 Relationship to Secular and International Law; Reinforcing Character

The Ethical-Use Conditions are primarily a **reinforcing and remedial layer**. No greater
enforceability is claimed than the forum's law grants; structural and technical safeguards,
not this License, are the primary protection; this License is the fallback.

**Exception, and its honest statement.** The core *Riba* prohibition of §C.9 is asserted as a
substantive condition of the grant to the maximum extent any forum will enforce it, **and** as
a condition of representation and association in every forum regardless of enforceability —
see [§C.9.5](#c95-charter-level-refusal-independent-of-forum). Because a reader could not
otherwise tell which conditions operate how, the Steward publishes a per-clause statement of
enforcement posture at [`/license/enforceability/`](enforceability-two-track-table.md). That
publication is required by *Sidq* and is not optional presentation.

The two-of-five test governs disputes concerning Works **not on the List**; for Works on the
List, the List is the operative designation. Both mechanisms are needed and they compose: the
List supplies predictability to a party evaluating adoption, and the test bounds the fallback
so that it cannot swallow all documentation.

Software attribution is a NOTICE file. Foundational works need more, because the harm is not
an uncredited copy but a misdirected one — and civil-law jurisdictions supply this by statute

## PART B — RECIPROCITY AND DISTRIBUTION

### B.6 Network Use and Provision of Functionality

Carried forward unchanged (the objective-test trigger via §2.2; the obligation in B.6.2; the
express exclusions in B.6.3; anti-circumvention in B.6.4), with B.6.5 as follows.

**B.6.5 Sensitive-Material Redaction — Minimal and Justified.** The source-availability
obligation does not require disclosure of private keys, user data, or genuine operational
secrets whose disclosure would itself cause harm (*Darar*) or breach a trust (*Amanah*).
However, any such redaction must be (a) **minimal** — confined to the specific material whose
disclosure would cause the harm; (b) **justified** — accompanied by a published, good-faith
statement identifying the *category* of material redacted and the harm avoided, without
revealing the secret itself; and (c) **non-defeating** — it must not impair the recipient's
ability to study, build, and run an equivalent version. Over-redaction, or invoking
"operational secrets" as a pretext to withhold material that is not genuinely sensitive, is a
circumvention of *Shafafiyah* and a breach under §B.5 and §9. **The burden of justifying a
redaction rests on the party making it.**

This is the same minimal-and-justified discipline applied to ON REQUEST assessment evidence in
[MX-005 §5.4](../corpus/mx-005-credentialing-and-assessment.md#54-evidence-and-on-request-material) —
one standard, two surfaces.

### C.9 — Absolute Prohibition of Core Riba

**C.9.1 Status.** This Section establishes the single elevated absolute prohibition of this
License. It is a **non-severable condition of every grant** (subject to the reformation rule
in §C.9.6) and a **charter-level refusal** under Part G. It is read together with, and as the
sole exception to the breadth of permission in, §C.8. **Nothing outside the bounded definition
in §C.9.2 is reached by this Section.**

**C.9.2 Definition of Core Riba.** "**Core Riba**" means a transaction or instrument bearing
**all** of the following cumulative elements:

> **(a) A monetary or fungible-of-the-same-kind base.** The dealing concerns money, currency,
> or a fungible commodity exchanged for more of the same kind, or a loan or advance of money
> or such fungible; **and**
>
> **(b) A pre-fixed, guaranteed increase.** The provider is entitled, by the structure of the
> dealing, to a **predetermined increase over the principal** that is fixed or determinable in
> advance and **guaranteed irrespective of the actual outcome** of any underlying real
> economic activity; **and**
>
> **(c) Absence of genuine risk-bearing or value-creating counter-performance.** The increase
> is **not** the return on a genuine, risk-bearing investment, nor the price or profit of a
> real asset, service, or value actually transferred, produced, or borne by the provider; the
> provider assumes no genuine commercial risk of loss of principal arising from the venture,
> and performs no real economic activity commensurate with the increase. The increase accrues
> by virtue of **deferral, delay, or the mere lending of money** (*riba al-nasī'ah*), or by
> virtue of an **unequal exchange of the same fungible kind** (*riba al-faḍl*); **and**
>
> **(d) Extraction from a counterparty.** The increase is extracted from a borrower or
> counterparty, transferring value to the provider without corresponding risk borne or value
> given.

A transaction is Core Riba **only if all of (a)–(d) are present together.** The absence of any
one element removes the transaction from this Section entirely, whereupon §C.8's general
permission governs.

**C.9.3 What Core Riba is NOT (express exclusions, for certainty — *Adl*).** The following are
**not** Core Riba and are **not** prohibited: (i) **profit** from genuine trade — buying and
selling real assets at a margin; (ii) **rent or lease** income on a real asset whose risk the
provider bears; (iii) **profit- and loss-sharing** returns where the provider genuinely bears
risk of loss; (iv) **fees for actual services** genuinely rendered — administration, custody,
validation, processing — priced for the service and not as a disguised time-value-of-money
charge; (v) **yield, staking, or validation rewards** compensating genuine effort, service, or
risk rather than the mere lending of value; (vi) **demurrage or negative-rate mechanisms** that
are charges for holding or storage, not increases extracted on a loan; (vii) any return where
the provider bears genuine risk of loss of principal from the underlying venture.

Where a structure is ambiguous as between Core Riba and a permitted form, and a good-faith,
risk-bearing, value-creating construction is available, **that construction is presumed and the
transaction is permitted.** The presumption operates **only at the boundary** and never to
dissolve a transaction that squarely meets all of (a)–(d).

A non-binding **safe harbour** of pre-classified ordinary fact patterns is published at
[`/license/riba/safe-harbour/`](determinations-procedure.md#4-the-riba-safe-harbour) so that a
licensee's counsel need not reconstruct the §C.9.2 analysis from first principles for every
ordinary payment integration. The greatest adoption risk this License faces is not
disagreement with its ethics but the cost of determining compliance, and the safe harbour
exists to lower that cost.

**C.9.4 Refusal.** A Work may not be used, nor knowingly permitted to be used (knowledge
standard per §C.6), to operate, issue, enforce, or principally facilitate **Core Riba** as the
designed and intended function of that use. The License grants **no rights** for such use; it
is outside the scope of the grant, void, and a **material, non-curable breach** under §9.3.

**C.9.5 Charter-Level Refusal Independent of Forum.** Distinct from the rest of Part C, which
§1.5 frames as reinforcing, the Steward elects that the refusal of Core Riba is asserted at the
charter level (Part G) as a matter of this License's own normative order, **whether or not** any
particular forum would independently enforce it. A Work or Derived License whose designed
purpose is the operation of Core Riba is **non-conforming to the Charter** and may not be
represented as governed by, compatible with, or derived from this License.

The honest reading, stated because a licensee cannot otherwise tell: §C.9 is a condition of the
grant **where enforceable**, and a condition of **association and representation everywhere**.
Even where a forum will not enforce the refusal as a limit on use, it remains enforceable as a
limit on the right to call the work ML-licensed — a representation claim available in nearly
every jurisdiction. That right is the sanction.

**C.9.6 Non-Severability, corrected.** *[Revised — Version 2.1.]*

> §C.9 may not be removed, weakened, waived, or contracted around by any party by agreement,
> distribution, sublicense, relicense, dual-license, combination, or Derived License, and any
> purported private instrument doing so is void and conveys no rights. **Judicial reform is
> treated differently:** if a court of competent jurisdiction finds §C.9 unenforceable in whole
> or in part as a condition of use in that forum, §C.9 shall be reformed to the narrowest form
> that forum will enforce, and the remainder of the grant survives. In any forum where §C.9
> cannot be enforced as a limit on use in any form, it remains fully operative as a limit on
> **representation and association** under §C.9.5 and §G.4, which the parties agree is
> severable from and independent of the use limitation.

The correction matters. Under ML-2.0's flat non-severability, a court finding §C.9
unenforceable would collapse the entire grant, stripping a licensee of the License for reasons
unconnected to their conduct — a disproportionate outcome and itself a failure of *Adl*. The
revision separates the two things the earlier clause conflated: private waiver, which remains
absolutely prohibited, and judicial reform, which is permitted to the narrowest enforceable
form.

### C.10 — Machine Learning, Model Training, and Synthetic Derivation

*[New — Version 2.1. This Section is the sole operative text on machine reading anywhere in
the MetaX corpus. Every other statement on the subject, including
[MX-002 §2.6](../corpus/mx-002-terms-of-service.md#26-machine-reading-and-ai-training), is a

> **C.10.1 Permitted training.** Use of the Work as training, fine-tuning, retrieval, or
> evaluation data for a machine-learning system is **permitted**, and is not by itself a
> breach of any Condition. The Steward affirms that learning from a Work is an act of study,
> which §0.3 preserves as a core liberty, and that a general prohibition on machine reading
> would be a prohibition on a class of *reader* rather than a class of *conduct* — which *Adl*
> does not permit.
>
> **C.10.2 Provenance obligation (Sidq).** Where a Work of Foundational Character has been
> used as training or retrieval data for a system made available to third parties, You must, to
> the extent technically practicable, (a) retain and expose the Work's identity in the
> system's disclosed data provenance, and (b) not configure the system to present material
> substantially derived from the Work as originating elsewhere or as unattributed. Where the
> system supports source attribution at inference, the Work's canonical identifier must be
> attributable.
>
> **C.10.3 Substantial reproduction.** Model outputs that reproduce a substantial portion of
> the Work's expression are Derivatives for the purposes of Part B and carry its obligations.
> Outputs that reflect only patterns, facts, methods, or ideas learned from the Work are not
> Derivatives, and this License asserts no claim over them. The line is the ordinary copyright
> line; this Section neither extends nor narrows it.
>
> **C.10.4 Anti-laundering.** It is a breach of *Sidq* and *Amanah*, and a Condition breach
> under Part C, to use a machine-learning system for the principal purpose of stripping
> attribution, provenance, or version identity from the Work, or of producing an unattributed
> functional or expressive equivalent in order to escape this License. The knowledge standard
> of §C.6 applies.
>
> **C.10.5 Impersonation of the Licensor.** No system trained on or retrieving from a Work of
> Foundational Character may be presented as speaking for, as the voice of, or as authorised
> by the Licensor or Steward, absent express written permission. Systems may state that they
> were trained on or can cite the Work.
>
> **C.10.6 No bar on defensive use.** Nothing in this Section restricts the use of the Work in
> machine-learning research whose purpose is the study, criticism, auditing, or falsification
> of the Work itself. Such use is expressly encouraged (*Shafafiyah*).

**Summary-required subset.** For the purposes of the single-source rule in
[GM-01 §3](../00-governance-map/precedence-and-conflict-rules.md#3-summaries-never-govern), any
permitted summary of this Section must carry at minimum: training permitted; provenance

represent itself as based on this License.

**(d)** A Derived License **must preserve §C.9 in full and unweakened** (§C.9.6), including the
reformation rule as drafted.

**(e)** A Derived License must state honestly and conspicuously that it is "based on Maher's
License, Version 2.1", identify what it adds or changes, carry its own distinct name and

**(g)** *[New — Version 2.1.]* A Derived License must preserve §C.10.2 (provenance) and §C.10.5
(impersonation) unweakened, and must preserve §6-bis(c) (version identity).

### E.7 — Registry Continuity and Canonical Text Redundancy

*[New — Version 2.1.]*

> **(a) Mirrors.** The Steward shall maintain the canonical text, version history, and all
> published Determinations in at least three independent, publicly readable locations, of which
> at least one shall be a content-addressed or cryptographically verifiable store and at least
> one shall be a jurisdictionally distinct archive not under the Steward's operational control.
>
> **(b) Signing.** Each released version and each published Determination shall be signed by
> the Steward's published key, and the key's fingerprint shall be reproduced in the canonical
> text.
>
> **(c) Failure of the Registry.** If the Registry is unreachable for 180 consecutive days, the
> most recent signed mirror is the canonical text for all purposes, and Part D and Part G
> Determinations are suspended — existing Determinations continue in force; no new ones may be
> issued — until continuity is restored under §E.8.
>
> **(d) Reliance.** No licensee loses rights by reason of the Steward's failure to maintain the
> Registry. Ambiguity arising from Registry failure is resolved in favour of the licensee.

Operational form: [Registry and Mirrors](registry-and-mirrors.md). A license that depends on
one domain resolving is not durable on a fifty-year horizon.

### E.8 — Steward Succession and Incapacity

*[New — Version 2.1.]*

> **(a) Named successor.** The Steward shall maintain, in the Registry, a published succession
> instrument naming at least one successor Steward and at least one alternate, together with
> the conditions of accession.
>
> **(b) Incapacity.** Upon the Steward's death, adjudicated incapacity, or 365 days of
> unexplained inactivity in the Registry, the named successor accedes automatically upon
> publishing a signed accession notice.
>
> **(c) Failure of succession.** If no successor accedes within 180 days of a triggering event,
> stewardship of Part D and Part G lapses, and the License continues in force as a direct
> license under Parts A, B, C, E and F only. **Lapse does not terminate any grant.** Existing
> Determinations remain published as historical record and cease to be capable of amendment.
>
> **(d) Successor constraint.** A successor Steward may issue new versions but may not weaken
> the Foundational Principles, §C.9, or §G.5. A purported version doing so is not a version of
> this License.
>
> **(e) Anti-capture.** Stewardship may not be transferred as an asset in a transaction whose
> predominant purpose is commercial control of the licensed corpus. Transfer must be to a
> person or body that accepts the Charter Conditions in writing and publishes that acceptance.

Clause (c) is deliberately unglamorous and is the important one. A license that dies with its
Steward strands every Work under it. Letting the charter machinery lapse while the direct grant
survives means the corpus outlives the institution, which is the correct priority.

Operational form: [Steward Succession](steward-succession.md). The parallel instruments for the
Curator and for the agent layer are
[MX-015](../corpus/mx-015-succession-and-continuity.md) and
[AG-000 §A.6](../agents/ag-000-agent-constitution.md#a6-degraded-mode-and-prompter-succession).

## PART G — THE CHARTER LAYER

### G.1 Purpose and Supremacy

This Part supplies the top-level governing order for Works of Foundational Character (§2.2) and
for all Derived Licenses. Within the scope of this License, the Charter Layer is **supreme over
the direct-license layer**: where a Work functions to establish or govern an order, the manner
of that establishment must itself conform to the Foundational Principles. The Charter Layer does
not enlarge the legal claims of Part C beyond §1.5 — save for the elected charter-level refusal
of Core Riba in §C.9.5 — but states the normative standard to which foundational Works and
derived instruments must conform to be entitled to invoke this License.

### G.2 Charter Conditions for Works of Foundational Character

A Work of Foundational Character governed by this License must, in its own substance and
operation:

**(a) Truthful establishment (Sidq).** Not establish or propagate, as foundational, claims known
by the author to be false, nor misrepresent the basis, authorship, or authority of the order it
establishes; and label its own character honestly, including under §0.2.

**(b) Just ordering (Adl).** Not order knowledge, value, or conduct in a manner designed to
entrench unjust advantage, oppression (*Zulm*), or the exploitation of those subject to the
order.

**(c) Transparent foundation (Shafafiyah).** Make the basis of the order it establishes
available for study and good-faith critique by those subject to it, to the extent consistent
with lawful confidentiality and §B.6.5.

**(d) Trustworthy stewardship (Amanah).** Be stewarded as a trust for those it governs, not as
an instrument of private capture against them; and pass stewardship only to those who will
maintain it consistent with these Conditions (cf. §E.5, §E.8).

**(e) Non-harm (Darar) and non-Riba (§C.9).** Not be designed to inflict unlawful harm, and not
be designed to operate, mandate, or principally facilitate Core Riba.

### G.3 Charter Conformity Determinations

The Steward may issue, by the same reasoned, published, good-faith procedure as Part D (§D.4–D.5
applied *mutatis mutandis*), Charter Conformity Determinations classifying a Work of
Foundational Character or a Derived License as **Charter-Conforming** or
**Charter-Non-Conforming**, with a written statement of reasons mapped to §G.2 and a
pros-and-cons record. Such Determinations are advisory classifications within this License's
framework, are not legal judgments, and bind only the entitlement to invoke and be associated
with this License.

### G.4 Consequence of Non-Conformity

A Work or Derived License determined Charter-Non-Conforming may not represent itself as governed
by, compatible with, derived from, or endorsed by this License or its Steward, and any rights
purportedly granted under this License for the non-conforming aspect are void to that extent.
Non-conformity of one aspect does not void rights validly exercised in good-faith reliance as
to conforming aspects (cf. §E.4 reliance protection and §14 severability). **Determinations
operate prospectively; see §G.6.**

### G.5 Humility Clause

Consistent with the Foundational Principles, the Steward acknowledges that the Charter Layer is
itself a human instrument open to error and to good-faith critique and improvement. A Charter
Conformity Determination must engage critique on its merits, must afford the affected party a
fair opportunity to respond, and must be revised upon sound correction. **No Determination may
be issued in bad faith, for the suppression of legitimate dissent, or to entrench the Steward's
advantage against those the Charter is meant to serve** — for that would itself violate *Adl*
and *Amanah* and would render the Determination void.

This clause binds the Steward against himself and may not be weakened by any successor
(§E.8(d)). It is the provision that makes every other provision credible, and it is not to be
amended.

### G.6 — Prospectivity and Cure

*[New — Version 2.1.]*

> A Charter Conformity Determination operates prospectively from the date of publication. The
> affected party has 90 days from publication to cure or to publish a reasoned response, during
> which the entitlements in §G.4 are not affected. Copies distributed and rights exercised in
> good faith before publication are unaffected. Where a party publishes a reasoned response,
> the Steward must issue a reasoned reply engaging its merits before the Determination takes
> effect.

Without this Section a licensee could not determine whether a Determination operated
retroactively against copies already distributed, which would make the License commercially
unusable.
