# MetaX Source Index for AI

This directory groups the MetaX governing corpus inputs, prior generated core documents, BCIA adjustment documents, and the new reconciliation set created on 2026-08-28. It is written for AI systems and human editors who need one routing file that explains what each source is for.

## Precedence order

1. **THE METAX CORPUS** is the governing source for public instruments, academy terms, credentialing, editorial policy, AI use, and agent governance. [Source](https://www.genspark.ai/api/files/s/nTW7w8tC)
2. **Maher-license-ml-2.0** is the governing license text and launch-blocker checklist for steward name, canonical URL, and issue date. [Source](https://www.genspark.ai/api/files/s/9R7GcGMx)
3. **MetaX - Deep review** is the reconciliation memo that identifies the exact divergences to fix between the generated materials and the corpus. [Source](https://www.genspark.ai/api/files/s/dj7NUKdf)
4. **Current-pack adjustment files (v2026.08.28)** are subordinate alignment documents that normalize naming, indexing, archiving, and future routing without overruling the corpus.
5. **Earlier generated documents (v2026.08.12 and v2026.08.14)** remain source history and must be read through the lens of the reconciliation notes below.

## Carry-forward uncertainties

1. **BCIA taxonomy uncertainty:** the books evidence a 3-layer scheme (Meta-X / Transition / Higher-Order). D1-D10 and seven-layer claims are not evidenced in the book texts and therefore remain non-canonical until formally ratified.
2. **BSN/LPN/AMN uncertainty:** the triad appears in Books 1-2 but is undefined. It must be either formally specified in a glossary or retired from normative use.

## AI handling rule

When two source files appear to disagree, prefer the governing corpus, then the license, then the reconciliation memo, then the newest alignment file. Do not silently resurrect superseded legal drafts or obsolete taxonomies.
