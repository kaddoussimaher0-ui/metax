⊢ MX-001 §1.4 · The standard

One thing is assessed: judgement under scrutiny.

A MetaX credential asserts that a named reviewer examined a real artefact produced by the candidate, against a published rubric, in a process the candidate could see — and could defend the resulting decision to a second calibrated reviewer who had not seen the first reviewer's reasoning.

It asserts nothing about employability, income, or aptitude. Any page on any MetaX property that implies otherwise is a defect and should be reported under MX-016.
