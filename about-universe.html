What MetaX is.

MetaX is a professional and scientific education estate built on one constraint: every substantive claim carries the information a stranger needs in order to find out that it is wrong. Not education trusted because of who published it — education that can be checked.

The Properties → Read the Charter

This is a narrower ambition than it sounds, and a more demanding one: it means MetaX must publish its own limits, defects and unresolved questions continuously — as a condition of publishing anything at all.
